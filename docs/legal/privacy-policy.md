# BrodaMeko Privacy Policy
Last updated: March 12, 2026

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how BrodaMeko collects, uses, stores, and shares personal data when you use our mobile application and related services.

## 2. Information We Collect
- Account data: name, email address, phone number, role, profile image.
- Identity/profile data: role-specific setup details (for example mechanic or seller profile setup inputs).
- Transactional data: job/order records, quotation actions, wallet and payout metadata.
- Messaging/notification data: chat metadata, notification preferences, push token/device registration metadata.
- Technical data: device/app diagnostics, crash and usage logs needed for service reliability.
- Location/media permissions data: location, camera, and file-access related data when required for enabled features.

## 3. How We Use Information
- Create and manage user accounts.
- Enable role-based features (car owner, mechanic, spare parts seller).
- Process wallet, escrow, marketplace, and payout-related workflows.
- Deliver push/in-app notifications and support communications.
- Prevent abuse, fraud, and unauthorized access.
- Diagnose issues and improve performance, reliability, and feature quality.

## 4. Sharing and Disclosure
We may share data with:
- Payment and payout providers for transaction processing.
- Infrastructure and communication service providers (for notifications, messaging, storage, and analytics).
- Regulatory or legal authorities when required by law or lawful process.

We do not sell personal data.

## 5. Permissions
Depending on feature usage, BrodaMeko may request:
- Location permission: for map and live-tracking flows.
- Camera/media/storage permission: for avatar, verification, and image uploads.
- Notification permission: to deliver activity and transactional alerts.

## 6. Data Retention
We retain data for operational, legal, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and compliance reasons. Retention periods may vary by data category and legal requirements.

## 7. Security
We use reasonable administrative, technical, and operational safeguards to protect data. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

## 8. Your Choices
You may:
- Update profile information in-app where supported.
- Manage device permissions via OS settings.
- Request account-related actions through support channels.

## 9. Children’s Privacy
BrodaMeko is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law. Accounts found to violate this may be restricted.

## 10. International Processing
Your data may be processed in jurisdictions where our providers operate, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

## 11. Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updated versions take effect when published in-app or in our documentation.

## 12. Contact
For privacy questions, contact BrodaMeko support through the in-app support channel.
